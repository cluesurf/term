# verb-pair's rows for the takes it cannot generate (decision cli-in-term/013 part 2, task/term/verb-pair.ts).
#
# A boolean take is generated as its flag alone, and a take with choices as each choice and one value outside them. Every
# other take (a text, a number, a list, a slot) is a row here, in verb-pair.txt's format: `<fixture> [NAME=value ...]
# <arguments>`. A row covers every take its words pass, and is named for the first. A take that cannot run here is
# `skip <verb path> --<take> <reason>` (network, publishing, signing, a device, or a run with no exit), which also stops
# a boolean's or a choice's generated row. A take with neither stops verb-pair before any run.
#
# The values are chosen to reach the handler and come back fast: a refusal of a bad value is as good a witness as a
# build, since a take the console drops answers as if it were never given.
